**MKCinema** is a free home cinema app for the whole family, on every screen you own: web, iPhone and iPad, Android, Android TV and Fire TV, Windows, Mac, Linux, and LG and Samsung TVs. One person builds all of it.

- **Watch:** an in-browser player that plays MKV, Dolby and DTS sound and HEVC, skips intros by itself and writes AI captions on your device.
- **Discover:** a Top 10 for any country, network or year, Your Picks worked out on your device, Peak Cinema and 142 Universes.
- **Together:** friends, messages and calls that wait for the credits, and watch parties with voice and cameras.
- **The whole family:** a seat for everyone, kids seats, Family Helper, and Miko, an assistant on every screen.
- **Free forever:** no ads, no paywalls, no account needed.
